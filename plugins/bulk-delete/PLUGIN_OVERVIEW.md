Select several threads in bb's sidebar and delete or archive them in one go, and clean up finished subagents of a parent thread.

## What you get

- Multi-select in the sidebar: Shift+click a row (or pick "Select" in its menu) to start; while a selection exists a click toggles a row, Shift+click selects a range, Escape clears.
- A floating bar with Archive and Delete for the selection. Delete shows exactly which threads go, and can include their child threads.
- "Delete idle children…" in a parent thread's menu: deletes every child that is not working and keeps the rest.
- `bb bulk-delete children` and `bb bulk-delete threads` for the same from a terminal or an agent.
