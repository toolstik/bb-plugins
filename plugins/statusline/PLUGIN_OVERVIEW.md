Statusline puts a compact status bar above the composer. It shows context window usage in percent and tokens, plus actual usage and reset times for provider limits. The context is green below 65%, yellow from 65% to below 80%, and red from 80%. Hover a segment for exact numbers.

With at least 10% actual usage, the limit meter follows the forecast when available and actual usage otherwise. The meter is green through 80%, yellow above 80% and below 98%, and red from 98%. The forecast indicator shows a yellow triangle or red circle for the corresponding warning zone. The meter stays green below 10% actual usage. Hover for projected usage at reset and any expected excess. Forecasts apply to five-hour, daily, and weekly limits with known reset times.
